# Northstar Health Network

Northstar Health Network is a fictional healthcare organization
created solely for portfolio analysis.

No asset, hostname, IP address, patient record, employee record,
or infrastructure configuration represents a real organization.

## Locations

- Northstar Central Hospital
- Northstar Riverside Hospital
- 8 outpatient clinics
- Corporate administration
- Remote workforce

## Approximate Infrastructure

- 650 employee workstations
- 150 clinical workstations
- 120 servers
- 40 network/security devices
- 25 internet-facing systems
- 15 medical/clinical technology systems

Approximate total:
1,000 assets

## Business Criticality

Assets will be classified as:

1 - Low
2 - Moderate
3 - Important
4 - High
5 - Mission Critical

## Exposure

Assets will also include:

- Internet exposed: Yes/No
- Patient-care supporting: Yes/No
- Contains sensitive data: Yes/No
- Production: Yes/No
