# Northstar Health Vulnerability Remediation Intelligence

## Business Problem

Northstar Health operates hospitals, clinics, employee endpoints,
servers, network infrastructure and internet-facing systems.

The security team discovers more vulnerabilities than it can
remediate immediately.

Traditional prioritization based primarily on CVSS severity can
result in remediation effort being spent on severe vulnerabilities
that present less immediate organizational risk while exploitable
or exposed vulnerabilities remain unresolved.

## Primary Question

Given limited remediation capacity, which vulnerabilities should
Northstar Health remediate first to reduce the greatest amount of
organizational cyber risk?

## Supporting Questions

1. Which vulnerabilities affect the most critical assets?
2. Which vulnerabilities are known to be actively exploited?
3. Which vulnerabilities have the highest exploitation probability?
4. Which internet-facing assets have urgent vulnerabilities?
5. Which business units carry the greatest vulnerability exposure?
6. How old are unresolved vulnerabilities?
7. How much risk could be reduced under different patching capacities?
8. Does prioritizing by organizational risk outperform CVSS-only prioritization?
